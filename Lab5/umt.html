About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia. It was officially chartered on 1 February 2007. The university focuses on marine science and aquatic resources, reflecting its strategic location along the South China Sea coast. UMT's commitment to these fields makes it a premier institution for maritime studies in the region.

The university began as the Fisheries and Marine Science Centre of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (now Universiti Putra Malaysia) in 1979. Over the years, it evolved through several structural changes, eventually becoming University College of Science and Technology Malaysia (KUSTEM) before officially achieving full university status as UMT. Today, it hosts a vibrant community of students and researchers dedicated to sustainable development and marine conservation.
